NOVELTRIA International Trade Services is an intermediation house specializing in international trade, initiated by its founder Laurent Huiskens. Its mission is to support enterprises in their global sourcing, commercial negotiation under mandate, and identification of export opportunities. The project is currently under constitution.

Unlike traditional brokers who may receive undisclosed commissions from both parties and create gray areas, NOVELTRIA operates strictly under an exclusive written mandate: we act either exclusively on behalf of the buyer (procurement mandate) or the seller (sales mandate). This ensures complete contractual loyalty and eliminates any conflict of interest.

Category A encompasses our direct expertise services (sourcing, commercial negotiation, export markets) performed directly by NOVELTRIA under mandate. Category B covers logistics, customs formalities, transport insurance, and quality control, which are coordinated on a tailored basis with licensed third-party specialists, as NOVELTRIA does not directly engage in these regulated professions.

Our confirmed operational sectors cover Textile & Apparel, Electronics & Components, and Industrial Materials & Raw Commodities. Any other industry is subject to a preliminary eligibility assessment with our team.

Every identified supplier undergoes a rigorous documentary audit: official registration, operating and industrial conformity licenses (ISO, CE), financial standing, verified manufacturing capacity, and physical compliance of production samples before any contractual or financial commitment.

No. NOVELTRIA is neither a freight carrier nor a registered customs broker. However, within our Category B services, we advise you on selecting the most appropriate Incoterms 2020 rule and coordinate seamlessly with certified freight forwarders and customs representatives.

We work with SMEs and mid-caps aiming to secure and diversify their global procurement channels or export to new markets, as well as established industrial corporations seeking competitive, reliable purchasing alternatives.

Our fee structure is transparently agreed upon in the preliminary mandate contract. Depending on project complexity, it can combine an upfront retainer and/or a success-based commission calculated on negotiated transaction volumes or realized cost savings.

Confidentiality is central to our professional ethics. Prior to any disclosure of technical specifications, sensitive blueprints, or commercial strategies, a strict bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is systematically executed between both parties.

NOVELTRIA International Trade Services is a project under constitution led by its founder, Laurent Huiskens. This upfront transparency is an integral part of our standard of professional rigor.
