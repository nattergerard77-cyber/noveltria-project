Official Service Catalogue

Structured Solutions to Secure Your Global Trade

A strict demarcation between our direct expertise and third-party logistics support

NOVELTRIA ensures total transparency regarding our operational scope: we clearly distinguish direct intermediation services conducted under written mandate (Category A) from our logistics assistance coordinated with qualified third parties (Category B).

Core Business Expertise

Category A — Intermediation & Direct Mandate

These missions are conducted directly by NOVELTRIA International Trade Services. They are governed by a formal written mandate contract, guaranteeing exclusive loyalty to your commercial interests and the absolute absence of conflicts of interest.

Supplier Sourcing & Qualification

We identify and vet top-performing, solvent international manufacturers capable of meeting your exact technical specifications and quality requirements.

  • Methodical identification of competitive manufacturing hubs.
  • Preliminary verification of operating licenses and actual capacities.
  • Management and technical validation of pre-production samples.
  • Comprehensive comparative dossiers covering pricing and factory terms.

Buyer Research & Export Markets

We actively prospect qualified buyers, distributors, and importers to unlock sustainable foreign markets for your products.

  • Mapping of target regional distributors and buying groups.
  • Qualification of buyer solvency and recurring order volumes.
  • Structured presentation of your industrial capabilities and catalog.
  • Direct introductions to senior commercial decision-makers.

Commercial Intermediation & Formalized Negotiation

We negotiate contract terms on your exclusive behalf to secure optimal margins, payment safety, and operational guarantees.

  • Rigorous unit pricing negotiations structured around volume tiers.
  • Securing firm shipment timelines and enforceable penalty clauses.
  • Safe international payment instruments (Letters of Credit, doc collections).
  • Drafting transparent, unambiguous commercial framework agreements.
Support & Coordination

Category B — Security & Third-Party Coordination

These services are proposed on a case-by-case basis to reinforce your transactions. NOVELTRIA does not directly perform regulated professions (carrier, customs broker, or insurance underwriter) but coordinates certified, accredited partners on your behalf.

Quality Control & Pre-shipment Inspection

Joint establishment of testing protocols with your team and coordination with independent inspection bodies to verify product conformity before embarkation under international AQL standards.

Multimodal International Transport

Strategic assistance in arbitrating between ocean shipping (FCL full containers or LCL consolidation), air cargo, and overland transit, with competitive bidding among reliable freight forwarders.

Customs Formalities & Incoterms 2020

Strategic advice on optimal Incoterm selection (FOB, CIF, DAP, DDP) to avoid costly clearance delays, and administrative interface with registered customs representatives.

Ad Valorem Marine Cargo Insurance

Advisory on carrier statutory liability limitations and introduction to marine insurance brokers to protect your goods up to 100% of their actual declared commercial value.

Methodology

Our 4-Stage Execution Process

A structured engagement model to guarantee the success of every trade intermediation mission.

Step 1

Scoping & NDA

Execution of a bilateral non-disclosure agreement and detailed formalization of your technical, financial, and scheduling requirements.

Step 2

Mandate Convention

Drafting and signing of the written mandate defining our exact negotiation delegation and agreed fee schedule.

Step 3

Sourcing & Negotiation

Active international market prospecting, factory qualification, commercial negotiations, and sample validation.

Step 4

Execution & Handover

Direct contractual execution between you and the selected partner, with coordination support for inspection and transport.
