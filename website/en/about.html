Origins & Ambition

At the Origins of NOVELTRIA

Contemporary international trade demands far more than basic introductions: it requires absolute discipline, acute understanding of manufacturing realities, and complete transparency at each link in the supply chain.

Initiated by Laurent Huiskens, NOVELTRIA International Trade Services was founded on a straightforward observation: faced with the increasing complexity of cross-border exchanges, organizations need a trusted partner capable of defending their interests exclusively during critical supplier negotiations and partner selection phases.

By placing ethical standards and rigorous methodology at the forefront, NOVELTRIA provides mid-sized enterprises and established industrial operators with the peace of mind required to secure their international import-export operations.

Ethics & Security

A Model Grounded in Exclusive Mandates

Why formalized written intermediation is the strongest protection for your financial and industrial interests.

Representation Without Conflict of Interest

Unlike opaque brokerage practices collecting commissions on both sides, NOVELTRIA operates strictly under mandate for a single principal: either the buyer or the seller. No conflicting incentives are accepted.

Trade Secret Protection (NDA)

Every assignment is preceded by the execution of a strict bilateral non-disclosure agreement. Your manufacturing formulas, technical drawings, procurement lists, and financial terms remain hermetically protected.

Traceability & Structured Reporting

You maintain full visibility throughout each prospecting phase. Detailed factory benchmarking dossiers and comprehensive pricing comparisons are delivered with rigorous analytical objectivity.
